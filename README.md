# PersonalEconomySystem
This system is made to help people attain a better insight into their own economy. By entering income, expences and fortune, the system will tell you what you have to do to become financially smarter.

With the system, the user can see how much equity they need to fund their expenses for the rest of their lives, based on the amount of luxury they want every year. 

With the system, it is also possible to privately log their fortune, to know exactly how much money they are worth.
